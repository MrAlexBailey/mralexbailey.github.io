Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Google Cloud project is used only by its owner to connect their own Google Nest devices (via the Smart Device Management API) to a Home Assistant server running on their home network.

What data is accessed

Device status, events (such as doorbell presses and motion), and camera streams from the owner's own Nest devices.

How it is used

Data is used only to display and automate the owner's devices within their private Home Assistant installation. It stays on the owner's own equipment.

Sharing

No data is collected by, sold to, or shared with any third party. There are no other users of this app.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
